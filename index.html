HOPDARE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

HOPDARE ("the Game") is developed by Danylo Rudenko ("we").

What we collect

The Game does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no login, no online leaderboards, no analytics and no advertising.

What is stored on your device

The Game saves your best scores, statistics, unlocked frogs and settings locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Sharing your result

When you tap "Share", the Game copies a short text with your result to the clipboard and saves a picture of it in the Game's own storage. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste or send it yourself.

Permissions

On Android the Game requests only the vibration permission, used for short haptic feedback.

Children

The Game is suitable for children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and contains no advertising.

Changes

If a future version adds purchases or any service that processes data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, describing the providers and data involved.
